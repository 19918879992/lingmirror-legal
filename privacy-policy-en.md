# Privacy Policy

LingMirror (hereinafter referred to as the "App") is a mobile language learning application that prioritizes local offline processing to minimize personal data uploads. This policy explains how we collect, use, and store your information.

Effective: 2026-11-05 · Updated: 2026-11-15

## 1. Information We Collect

1.1 **Audio recordings**: Microphone audio is captured in real time only when you start a shadowing practice. The audio is held in memory solely for real-time analysis and is released immediately once analysis completes — never saved, never uploaded.

1.2 **Device identifier**: Read from the app-scoped identifier provided by the system (identifierForVendor on iOS, ANDROID_ID on Android), or generated locally at random if the system does not provide one. It normally stays on your device and is compared locally to validate membership status and membership entitlements. When you take part in the cloud leaderboard or claim leaderboard rewards, the App uploads the device identifier together with your anonymous leaderboard ID to our cloud server, solely to associate your leaderboard rank with your device so that membership rewards can be granted to that device. Apart from these cases the identifier is not uploaded and is not shared with any third party. In addition, when you voluntarily copy and report the Device ID, you provide it to us for membership verification.

1.3 **App logs**: Used only to diagnose crashes. Not used for advertising.

1.4 We do NOT collect: contacts, precise location, SMS, call logs, health data, or the list of installed apps. The leaderboard avatar is a locally generated Emoji; the App does not access or upload your photo library and requests no camera permission.

## 2. Use of Information

2.1 **Local audio**: Used for on-device speech recognition and analysis of speaking rate, pauses, filler words and volume.

2.2 **Device ID**: Compared on-device locally to validate membership status and membership entitlements. When you take part in the cloud leaderboard or claim leaderboard rewards, it is uploaded together with your anonymous leaderboard ID, solely to associate your leaderboard rank with membership reward issuance; it is never used for advertising or profiling.

2.3 **Logs**: Used to fix bugs and improve the product.

## 3. Information Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent or trade your personal data with third parties. When you take part in the cloud leaderboard, we upload your anonymous ID, the nickname you set, an emoji avatar, your weekly points and the device identifier used to associate membership entitlements to our cloud server for leaderboard display, ranking and reward issuance; these data do not contain your real name, contact information, audio or precise location. Data is disclosed to third parties only when required by applicable law or by a competent authority in accordance with legal process.

## 4. Data Storage & Deletion

The App does not save any audio files; audio is released immediately after real-time analysis. No audio data is stored on our servers. The leaderboard avatar is an Emoji generated locally by the App and does not contain or access any of your photos. Local practice data (such as score records) remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. When you take part in the cloud leaderboard, only your anonymous ID, nickname, emoji avatar, weekly points and the device identifier used to associate membership entitlements are uploaded to the cloud, for leaderboard display, ranking and reward issuance. Cloud reward records (rank, reward days, claim status) and their binding to the device identifier are retained on our servers so that membership rewards can be settled across devices. The in-app messaging feature stores, keyed by device identifier, the titles, bodies, read status and creation time of system messages delivered to you, so that notifications can be shown inside the App; you may delete any single message or clear all messages in the App's "System Messages" page, after which the server no longer retains those message records. You may stop taking part in the leaderboard at any time in the App; once you do, new results are no longer uploaded. If you need us to delete cloud-stored leaderboard data or device-identifier bindings, you may contact us and we will assist upon verification.

## 5. Permissions

- **Microphone**: Used only to capture your pronunciation in real time for on-device analysis during shadowing practice; audio is never saved. Practice features cannot work without it.
- **Speech recognition**: Used to transcribe your shadowing pronunciation into text for word-by-word scoring. Both the Android and iOS versions use an on-device offline recognition engine (Vosk); neither the audio nor the recognition results are uploaded.
- **Notifications**: Used only for the persistent notification required by the foreground service during listening practice. You can disable them in system settings.
- **Foreground service and background playback**: Used to keep audio playing in the background during listening practice, so sound is not interrupted when you switch away from the App.
- The leaderboard avatar is generated locally as an Emoji and never reads your photo library; no camera permission is requested.
- The App does not request unnecessary permissions such as contacts or precise location.

## 6. Third-Party Services

6.1 **Apple App Store**: Used for processing subscription purchases and renewals. Payment information is handled directly by Apple; the developer cannot access your credit card number or billing address.

6.2 **CDN (Content Delivery Network)**: Used for downloading speech recognition models and practice content packages. This is publicly read-only resource retrieval that does not carry your personal information, recordings, or device identifier.

6.2a **Cloud leaderboard and reward storage (Cloudflare D1 database)**: When you take part in the cloud leaderboard or claim leaderboard rewards, we store the anonymous ID, nickname, emoji avatar and weekly points you upload, as well as the device identifier used to associate membership entitlements and the reward records (rank, reward days, claim status), for leaderboard display, ranking and membership reward settlement. No audio is stored. This processing is subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy.

6.3 **Vosk (on-device speech recognition engine)**: Both the Android and iOS versions run entirely offline on your device; audio never leaves the device.

6.4 **device_info_plus**: Reads only device model and OS version for UI layout adaptation; does not collect personally identifiable information.

6.5 **connectivity_plus**: Checks only network connection status; does not record network content.

6.6 **Local storage (sqflite / shared_preferences)**: Practice records and settings are stored locally on your device; not uploaded to servers.

6.7 **Audio playback and recording (audioplayers / record / flutter_tts)**: All processed locally; audio is not uploaded.

6.8 **File picker and share (file_picker / share_plus)**: Triggered only when you actively import a voice pack, change your avatar, or share a check-in result. Selected files are processed locally and are never uploaded to any server.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not save audio, and local practice data is processed on-device in real time. The cloud leaderboard involves only the anonymous ID, nickname, emoji avatar, weekly points, and the device identifier and reward records used to associate membership entitlements; it does not contain children's identity information, photos or audio. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the App without your consent, please contact us to describe the situation and we will do our best to assist and respond to your concerns.

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to delete your local practice data and avatar settings, to stop using the App, and to raise questions or complaints about this Policy. Local practice data can be deleted directly on your device; our servers only retain anonymous leaderboard data and the device identifier used to associate membership entitlements, which you may request us to delete via the contact details at the end of this Policy, and we will handle it upon verification.

## 9. Policy Updates

This Policy may be updated. Changes will be displayed on this page; material changes affecting your rights will be highlighted. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
