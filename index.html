一、概述 / 1. Overview

本隐私政策说明 LingMirror（以下简称"本应用"）如何收集、使用、存储您的信息。本应用优先采用本地离线运算，尽可能减少个人数据上传。

This Privacy Policy describes how LingMirror (the "App") collects, uses and stores your information. The App is designed for local offline processing to minimize personal data uploads.

二、我们收集的信息 / 2. Information We Collect

1. 录音数据：仅在您主动点击录制时采集麦克风音频。音频默认保存在您的本地设备，不会自动上传至服务器。

1. Audio recordings: Microphone audio is captured only when you manually start recording. Audio is stored locally on your device and is not automatically uploaded to any server.

2. 设备标识：本应用仅在本机生成设备标识，用于在本地与授权白名单比对（校验合作教学授权与会员权益是否有效）。该标识不会被上传至服务器；仅当您主动复制设备 ID 并报备给我们时，才会由您本人提供给我们用于授权审核。

2. Device identifier: Generated locally on your device and used only for local comparison against an authorization whitelist (to validate cooperative-teaching authorization and membership entitlements). It is never uploaded to our servers. Only when you voluntarily copy and report the Device ID do you provide it to us for authorization review.

3. 应用运行日志：仅用于排查程序崩溃问题，不用于广告推送。

3. App logs: Used only to diagnose crashes. Not used for advertising.

4. 我们不会收集：通讯录、精确位置、相册、短信、通话记录、健康数据、已安装应用列表等。

4. We do NOT collect: contacts, precise location, photos, SMS, call logs, health data, or the list of installed apps.

三、信息用途 / 3. Use of Information

• 本地音频：用于实时语音识别、语速、停顿、口头禅与音量分析；
• 设备标识：仅在本机生成并与授权白名单比对，用于校验合作教学授权与会员权益有效性，不上传服务器；
• 日志：用于修复缺陷、优化产品。

• Local audio: Used for on-device speech recognition and analysis of speaking rate, pauses, filler words and volume.
• Device ID: Generated and compared locally against the authorization whitelist to validate cooperative-teaching authorization and membership entitlements; never uploaded.
• Logs: Used to fix bugs and improve the product.

四、信息共享 / 4. Information Sharing

开发者不会向第三方出售、出租或交易您的录音及个人信息。仅在法律法规明确要求、或经有权机关依法调取时，才会按规定提交。

The developer does not sell, rent or trade your recordings or personal data with third parties. Data is disclosed only when required by applicable law or by a competent authority in accordance with legal process.

五、数据存储与删除 / 5. Data Storage & Deletion

所有录音文件保存在您的本机。您可以随时在应用内删除录音；卸载应用会清除本地相关数据。我们的服务器不存储您的语音录音。本地练习数据（评分记录等）保留在您的设备上，直至您主动删除或卸载应用。

All recordings are stored locally on your device. You may delete recordings within the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes the related local data. No voice recordings are stored on our servers. Local practice data (such as score records) remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

六、权限说明 / 6. Permissions

麦克风：仅用于录音与发音分析；无此权限将无法使用练习功能。
通知：仅用于练习提醒与下载进度提示，可在系统设置中关闭。
本应用不会请求通讯录、位置、相册等非必要权限。

Microphone: Required only for recording and pronunciation analysis. Practice features cannot work without it.
Notifications: Used only for practice reminders and download progress. You can disable them in system settings.
The App does not request unnecessary permissions such as contacts, location or photos.

七、第三方服务 / 7. Third-Party Services 重要

本应用使用以下第三方服务，其数据处理遵循各自的隐私政策：

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

• 应用商店结算服务（Google Play / Apple App Store）：用于处理订阅购买与续订。Android 版通过 Google Play 结算，iOS 版通过 Apple App Store 结算。支付信息由对应商店直接处理，开发者无法获取您的银行卡号或账单地址。
• 内容分发网络（CDN）：用于下载语音识别模型与练习内容数据包。下载请求为公开只读的资源获取，不携带您的个人信息、录音或设备标识。
• 本地语音识别引擎（Vosk）：完全在您的设备上离线运行，音频不会离开设备。

• App Store Billing (Google Play / Apple App Store): Processes subscription purchases and renewals. The Android version uses Google Play Billing; the iOS version uses Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment information is handled directly by the corresponding store; the developer never receives your card number or billing address.
• Content Delivery Network (CDN): Used to download speech-recognition models and practice content packages. These are public read-only resource requests and carry no personal information, recordings, or device identifiers.
• On-device speech recognition (Vosk): Runs entirely offline on your device. Audio never leaves the device.

八、儿童隐私 / 8. Children's Privacy

本应用不面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。若您是家长或监护人，发现孩子在未经您同意的情况下使用了本应用，请联系我们，我们会在核实后删除相关数据。

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the App without your consent, please contact us and we will delete the relevant data after verification.

九、您的权利 / 9. Your Rights

您有权删除本地录音与练习数据；有权停止使用本应用；有权就本政策向我们提出疑问或投诉。由于我们不集中存储您的个人数据，多数情况下删除操作可直接在您的设备上完成。

You have the right to delete your local recordings and practice data, to stop using the App, and to raise questions or complaints about this Policy. Because we do not centrally store your personal data, most deletion operations can be completed directly on your device.

十、政策更新 / 10. Policy Updates

本隐私政策可能更新。变更将在应用内展示；若变更涉及重大权利调整，我们会以显著方式提示。继续使用即代表接受更新后的政策。

This Policy may be updated. Changes will be displayed in the App; material changes affecting your rights will be highlighted. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
