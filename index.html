隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective: 2026-11-05  ·  最后更新 / Updated: 2026-11-12

一、概述 / 1. Overview

本隐私政策说明 LingMirror（以下简称"本应用"）如何收集、使用、存储您的信息。本应用优先采用本地离线运算，尽可能减少个人数据上传。

This Privacy Policy describes how LingMirror (the "App") collects, uses and stores your information. The App is designed for local offline processing to minimize personal data uploads.

二、我们收集的信息 / 2. Information We Collect

  • 1. 录音数据：仅在您主动开始跟读练习时实时采集麦克风音频。音频仅在内存中用于实时分析，分析完成后立即释放，不保存、不上传。
  • 2. 设备标识：本应用仅在本机生成设备标识，用于在本地与授权白名单比对（校验口语教学授权与会员权益是否有效）。该标识不会被上传至服务器；仅当您主动复制设备 ID 并报备给我们时，才会由您本人提供给我们用于授权审核。
  • 3. 应用运行日志：仅用于排查程序崩溃问题，不用于广告推送。
  • 4. 我们不会收集：通讯录、精确位置、短信、通话记录、健康数据、已安装应用列表等。本应用不会批量访问或上传您的相册；仅在您主动更换排行榜头像时，读取您所选中的那一张图片，该图片仅保存在本机，不会上传。
  • 1. Audio recordings: Microphone audio is captured in real time only when you start a shadowing practice. The audio is held in memory solely for real-time analysis and is released immediately once analysis completes — never saved, never uploaded.
  • 2. Device identifier: Generated locally on your device and used only for local comparison against an authorization whitelist (to validate spoken-language teaching authorization and membership entitlements). It is never uploaded to our servers. Only when you voluntarily copy and report the Device ID do you provide it to us for authorization review.
  • 3. App logs: Used only to diagnose crashes. Not used for advertising.
  • 4. We do NOT collect: contacts, precise location, SMS, call logs, health data, or the list of installed apps. The App does not access or upload your photo library in bulk; only when you actively change your leaderboard avatar does it read the single image you selected, and that image is stored locally and never uploaded.

三、信息用途 / 3. Use of Information

  • 本地音频：用于实时语音识别、语速、停顿、口头禅与音量分析；
  • 设备标识：仅在本机生成并与授权白名单比对，用于校验口语教学授权与会员权益有效性，不上传服务器；
  • 日志：用于修复缺陷、优化产品。
  • Local audio: Used for on-device speech recognition and analysis of speaking rate, pauses, filler words and volume.
  • Device ID: Generated and compared locally against the authorization whitelist to validate spoken-language teaching authorization and membership entitlements; never uploaded.
  • Logs: Used to fix bugs and improve the product.

四、信息共享 / 4. Information Sharing

我们不会向任何第三方出售、出租或交易您的个人信息。本应用实际涉及的信息范围有限，仅包括：设备标识（仅在本机用于口语教学授权核验）、本地练习数据（评分与积分记录，仅存于本机）、排行榜昵称与头像选择（仅存于本机）。上述信息均不会自动上传至我们的服务器。除法律法规明确要求、或经有权机关依法调取外，我们不会向任何第三方披露上述信息。

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data with any third party. The information involved in this App is limited to: the device identifier (used on-device only to validate spoken-language teaching authorization), local practice data (scores and points, stored on-device only), and your leaderboard nickname and avatar selection (stored on-device only). None of this information is uploaded automatically to our servers. We disclose it to third parties only when required by applicable law or by a competent authority in accordance with legal process.

五、数据存储与删除 / 5. Data Storage & Deletion

本应用不保存任何录音文件，音频在实时分析完成后立即释放。我们的服务器不存储您的语音录音。排行榜头像图片与本地练习数据（评分记录等）保留在您的设备上，直至您在应用内删除或卸载应用；卸载应用会清除全部本地相关数据。

The App does not save any audio recordings; audio is released immediately after real-time analysis. No voice recordings are stored on our servers. Leaderboard avatar images and local practice data (such as score records) remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App; uninstalling removes all related local data.

六、权限说明 / 6. Permissions

麦克风：仅用于跟读练习时实时采集发音并在本机分析，音频不保存；无此权限将无法使用跟读功能。
相册：仅在您主动更换排行榜头像时用于选择一张图片，图片仅保存在本机，不会上传。
相机：仅在选择头像时可能用于拍照，照片仅保存在本机，不会上传。
语音识别：用于将您的跟读发音识别为文字以完成逐词评分，识别在设备本地完成，录音与识别结果均不上传。
通知：仅用于练习提醒与下载进度提示，可在系统设置中关闭。
前台服务与后台播放：用于听力练习时后台持续播放音频，避免切出后声音中断。
本应用不会请求通讯录、精确位置等非必要权限。

Microphone: Used only to capture your pronunciation in real time for on-device analysis during shadowing practice; audio is never saved. Practice features cannot work without it.
Photos: Used only to select a single image when you actively change your leaderboard avatar; the image is stored locally and never uploaded.
Camera: May be used only to take a photo when setting your avatar; the photo is stored locally and never uploaded.
Speech recognition: Used to transcribe your shadowing pronunciation into text for word-by-word scoring. Recognition runs on-device; neither the audio nor the recognition results are uploaded.
Notifications: Used only for practice reminders and download progress. You can disable them in system settings.
Foreground service and background playback: Used to keep audio playing in the background during listening practice, so sound is not interrupted when you switch away from the App.
The App does not request unnecessary permissions such as contacts or precise location.

七、第三方服务 / 7. Third-Party Services

  • 应用商店结算服务（Apple App Store）：用于处理订阅购买与续订。支付信息由 Apple 直接处理，开发者无法获取您的银行卡号或账单地址。
  • 内容分发网络（CDN）：用于下载语音识别模型与练习内容数据包，为公开只读的资源获取，不携带您的个人信息、录音或设备标识。
  • 本地语音识别引擎（Vosk）：完全在您的设备上离线运行，音频不会离开设备。
  • 设备信息（device_info_plus）：仅读取设备型号与系统版本，用于适配界面布局，不收集个人身份信息。
  • 网络状态检测（connectivity_plus）：仅判断是否有网络连接，不记录网络内容。
  • 本地存储（sqflite / shared_preferences）：练习记录与设置保存在本机，不上传服务器。
  • 音频播放与录音（audioplayers / record / flutter_tts）：均在本地完成，音频不上传。
  • 文件选择与分享（file_picker / share_plus）：仅在您主动导入语音包、更换头像或分享打卡成果时触发，所选文件仅在本机处理，不上传服务器。
  • App Store Billing: Processes subscription purchases and renewals. Payment information is handled directly by Apple; the developer never receives your card number or billing address.
  • Content Delivery Network (CDN): Used to download speech-recognition models and practice content packages. These are public read-only resource requests and carry no personal information, recordings, or device identifiers.
  • On-device speech recognition (Vosk): Runs entirely offline on your device. Audio never leaves the device.
  • Device information (device_info_plus): Reads only the device model and OS version to adapt the interface layout; it does not collect personal identity information.
  • Network connectivity (connectivity_plus): Determines only whether a network connection exists; it does not record network content.
  • Local storage (sqflite / shared_preferences): Practice records and settings are stored on your device and are not uploaded to any server.
  • Audio playback and recording (audioplayers / record / flutter_tts): All run locally; audio is never uploaded.
  • File selection and sharing (file_picker / share_plus): Triggered only when you actively import a voice pack, change your avatar, or share a check-in result. Selected files are processed locally and are never uploaded to any server.

八、儿童隐私 / 8. Children's Privacy

本应用不面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。若您是家长或监护人，发现孩子在未经您同意的情况下使用了本应用，请联系我们，我们会在核实后删除相关数据。

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the App without your consent, please contact us and we will delete the relevant data after verification.

九、您的权利 / 9. Your Rights

您有权删除本地录音与练习数据；有权停止使用本应用；有权就本政策向我们提出疑问或投诉。由于我们不集中存储您的个人数据，多数情况下删除操作可直接在您的设备上完成。

You have the right to delete your local recordings and practice data, to stop using the App, and to raise questions or complaints about this Policy. Because we do not centrally store your personal data, most deletion operations can be completed directly on your device.
