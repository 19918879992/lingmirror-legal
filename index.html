隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective: 2026-11-05  ·  最后更新 / Updated: 2026-11-17

一、概述 / 1. Overview

本隐私政策说明 LingMirror（以下简称"本应用"）如何收集、使用、存储您的信息。本应用无需注册账号，不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱等注册信息。本应用的核心语音分析与练习数据均在本地完成，已尽可能减少个人数据上传。Android 版与 iOS 版语音识别均使用本地离线引擎（Vosk），详见第六节。

This Privacy Policy describes how LingMirror (the "App") collects, uses and stores your information. The App requires no account registration and does not collect registration information such as your name, phone number or email address. The App performs its core speech analysis and practice-data processing on-device, and has minimized personal data uploads as far as possible. Both the Android and iOS versions use an on-device offline speech-recognition engine (Vosk), as described in Section 6.

二、我们收集的信息 / 2. Information We Collect

  • 1. 音频数据：仅在您主动开始跟读练习时实时采集麦克风音频。音频仅在内存中用于实时分析，分析完成后立即释放，不保存为文件，也不会自动上传至服务器。
  • 2. 设备标识：为核验会员权益有效性，本应用会读取系统提供的应用级设备标识（iOS 为 identifierForVendor，Android 为 ANDROID_ID）；若系统未提供，则在本机随机生成一个标识。该标识平时仅保存在本机，用于在本机校验会员状态；当您参与云端排行榜或领取排行榜奖励时，本应用会将设备标识与您的排行榜匿名 ID 一并上传至我们的云端服务器，仅用于将排行榜名次与您的设备关联，以便向您的设备发放会员奖励。除上述情形外，该标识不会上传，也不会提供给任何第三方。此外，您主动复制设备 ID 并报备给我们时，由您本人提供该标识用于会员权益核验。
  • 3. 应用运行日志：仅用于排查程序崩溃问题，不用于广告推送。
  • 4. 我们不会收集：通讯录、精确位置、短信、通话记录、健康数据、已安装应用列表等。排行榜头像由系统在本地派生 Emoji 表情表示，本应用不会访问或上传您的相册，也不需要相机权限。
  • 1. Audio data: Microphone audio is captured in real time only when you start a shadowing practice. The audio is held in memory solely for real-time analysis, released immediately once analysis completes, never saved as a file, and never automatically uploaded to any server.
  • 2. Device identifier: To validate membership entitlements, the App reads the app-scoped device identifier provided by the system (identifierForVendor on iOS, ANDROID_ID on Android); if the system does not provide one, an identifier is generated locally at random. The identifier normally stays on your device and is compared locally to validate membership status. When you take part in the cloud leaderboard or claim leaderboard rewards, the App uploads the device identifier together with your anonymous leaderboard ID to our cloud server, solely to associate your leaderboard rank with your device so that membership rewards can be granted to that device. Apart from these cases the identifier is not uploaded and is not shared with any third party. In addition, when you voluntarily copy and report the Device ID, you provide it to us for membership verification.
  • 3. App logs: Used only to diagnose crashes. Not used for advertising.
  • 4. We do NOT collect: contacts, precise location, SMS, call logs, health data, or the list of installed apps. The leaderboard avatar is a locally generated Emoji; the App does not access or upload your photo library and requests no camera permission.

三、信息用途 / 3. Use of Information

本地音频：用于实时语音识别、语速、停顿、口头禅与音量分析；

设备标识：在本机校验会员状态，用于核验会员权益有效性；当您参与云端排行榜或领取排行榜奖励时，该标识与排行榜匿名 ID 一并上传，仅用于关联排行榜名次与会员权益发放，不用于广告投放或用户画像；

日志：用于修复缺陷、优化产品。

Local audio: Used for on-device speech recognition and analysis of speaking rate, pauses, filler words and volume.

Device ID: Compared on-device locally to validate membership status and membership entitlements. When you take part in the cloud leaderboard or claim leaderboard rewards, it is uploaded together with your anonymous leaderboard ID, solely to associate your leaderboard rank with membership reward issuance; it is never used for advertising or profiling.

Logs: Used to fix bugs and improve the product.

四、信息共享 / 4. Information Sharing

我们不会向任何第三方出售、出租或交易您的个人信息。本应用实际涉及的信息范围有限，仅包括：设备标识（在本机用于会员权益校验；参与排行榜或领取奖励时与排行榜匿名 ID 一并上传用于会员权益关联）、本地练习数据（评分与积分记录，仅存于本机）。当您参与云端排行榜时，我们会将匿名 ID、您设置的昵称、Emoji 头像、当周积分以及用于关联会员权益的设备标识上传至我们的云端服务器用于榜单展示、排名计算与奖励发放。该匿名 ID 由系统在本机随机生成，不包含您的真实姓名、联系方式、音频或精确位置；本应用也不会收集您的通讯录、短信等敏感信息。除法律法规明确要求、或经有权机关依法调取外，我们不会向任何第三方披露上述信息。

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data with any third party. The information involved in this App is limited to: the device identifier (used on-device to validate membership entitlements, and uploaded together with the anonymous leaderboard ID when you take part in the leaderboard or claim rewards, for membership entitlement association) and local practice data (scores and points, stored on-device only). When you take part in the cloud leaderboard, we upload an anonymous ID, the nickname you set, an emoji avatar, your weekly points and the device identifier used to associate membership entitlements to our cloud server for leaderboard display, ranking and reward issuance. The anonymous ID is generated randomly on your device and does not contain your real name, contact information, audio or precise location. The App does not collect your contacts, SMS or other sensitive information. We disclose this information to third parties only when required by applicable law or by a competent authority in accordance with legal process.

五、数据存储与删除 / 5. Data Storage & Deletion

本应用不保存任何音频文件，音频在实时分析完成后立即释放。我们的服务器不存储您的音频数据。排行榜头像为应用在本地生成的 Emoji 表情，不包含也不会访问您的照片；本地练习数据（评分记录等）保留在您的设备上，直至您在应用内删除或卸载应用。当您参与云端排行榜时，仅将匿名 ID、昵称、Emoji 头像、当周积分与用于关联会员权益的设备标识上传至云端，用于榜单展示、排名与奖励发放；这些数据不含您的姓名、联系方式、音频或精确位置。云端奖励记录（名次、奖励天数、领取状态）与设备标识的绑定关系会保留在服务器上，以便跨设备结算会员奖励。站内消息功能会按设备标识存储系统下发给您的消息标题、正文、阅读状态与创建时间，用于在应用内向您展示通知；您可在应用内「系统消息」页面自行删除单条消息或清空全部消息，删除后服务器不再保留该消息记录。您可随时在应用内停止参与排行榜，之后新的成绩将不再上传；如需处理云端已有的榜单数据与设备标识绑定记录，可联系我们协助，我们将在核实后按您的请求删除。

The App does not save any audio files; audio is released immediately after real-time analysis. No audio data is stored on our servers. The leaderboard avatar is an Emoji generated locally by the App and does not contain or access any of your photos. Local practice data (such as score records) remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. When you take part in the cloud leaderboard, only your anonymous ID, nickname, emoji avatar, weekly points and the device identifier used to associate membership entitlements are uploaded to the cloud, for leaderboard display, ranking and reward issuance; these data do not contain your real name, contact information, audio or precise location. Cloud reward records (rank, reward days, claim status) and their binding to the device identifier are retained on our servers so that membership rewards can be settled across devices. The in-app messaging feature stores, keyed by device identifier, the titles, bodies, read status and creation time of system messages delivered to you, so that notifications can be shown inside the App; you may delete any single message or clear all messages in the App's "System Messages" page, after which the server no longer retains those message records. You may stop taking part in the leaderboard at any time in the App; once you do, new results are no longer uploaded. If you need us to delete cloud-stored leaderboard data or device-identifier bindings, you may contact us and we will assist upon verification.

本地语音识别引擎（Vosk）：Android 版与 iOS 版均完全在您的设备上离线运行，音频不会离开设备。本应用不向自有服务器上传音频或识别结果。

屏幕常亮（wakelock_plus）：仅在跟读页面保持屏幕不自动熄灭，离开页面即恢复，不收集任何信息。

设备信息（device_info_plus）：仅读取设备型号与系统版本，用于适配界面布局，不收集个人身份信息。

网络状态检测（connectivity_plus）：仅判断是否有网络连接，不记录网络内容。

本地存储（sqflite / shared_preferences）：练习记录与设置保存在本机，不上传服务器。

音频播放与采集（audioplayers / record / flutter_tts）：均在本地完成，音频不上传。

文件选择与分享（file_picker / share_plus）：仅在您主动导入语音包、更换头像或分享打卡成果时触发，所选文件仅在本机处理，不上传服务器。

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

App Store Billing: Processes membership/subscription purchases and renewals. The iOS version is billed through Apple's App Store; any future version distributed via Google Play would be billed through Google Play. Payment information is handled directly by the corresponding store; the developer never receives your card number or billing address.

Content Delivery Network (CDN): Used to download speech-recognition models and practice content packages. These are public read-only resource requests and carry no personal information, audio, or device identifiers.

Cloud leaderboard and reward storage (Cloudflare D1 database): When you take part in the cloud leaderboard or claim leaderboard rewards, we store the anonymous ID, nickname, emoji avatar and weekly points you upload, as well as the device identifier used to associate membership entitlements and the reward records (rank, reward days, claim status), for leaderboard display, ranking and membership reward settlement. No audio is stored. This processing is subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy.

On-device speech recognition (Vosk): Both the Android and iOS versions run entirely offline on your device; audio never leaves the device. The App never uploads audio or recognition results to its own servers.

Screen wake lock (wakelock_plus): Keeps the screen from sleeping only on the shadowing page and restores it when leaving; it collects no information.

Device information (device_info_plus): Reads only the device model and OS version to adapt the interface layout; it does not collect personal identity information.

Network connectivity (connectivity_plus): Determines only whether a network connection exists; it does not record network content.

Local storage (sqflite / shared_preferences): Practice records and settings are stored on your device and are not uploaded to any server.

Audio playback and capture (audioplayers / record / flutter_tts): All run locally; audio is never uploaded.

File selection and sharing (file_picker / share_plus): Triggered only when you actively import a voice pack, change your avatar, or share a check-in result. Selected files are processed locally and are never uploaded to any server.

The App supports importing customization packs for partner creators such as speaking coaches and instructors: creators may prepare their own teaching materials and audio, package them into a customization pack recognized by the App, and after backend device authorization import them for local use. The terms are as follows:

Authorization and device binding

  • Permission to use a customization pack is subject to the developer's backend device allowlist: partners must first report their Device ID through out-of-app channels (email / form); imports are permitted only after the backend approves the device, and are rejected otherwise.
  • The Device ID is generated on-device with integrity verification to identify the same device. While the authorization remains valid, the device may import authorized packs at any time without reporting again.
  • Authorization may lapse due to expiry, revocation or suspension, after which imports of customization packs and related features will no longer be permitted.

Content and responsibility

  • Text, audio and other content inside customization packs are provided by the partner creator, who bears sole responsibility for their copyright and legality; partners must ensure they hold lawful rights to all such materials and must not upload infringing or unlawful content.
  • Customization packs are stored only in the local directory of the importing device and are never uploaded to the developer's servers.
  • Customization packs (including custom materials and audio) may be used by the partner only for teaching and sharing on authorized devices, and may not be transferred, resold for a fee, or further distributed to third parties.
